About the journal

One journal.
Many ways of knowing.

Shivraj 350: Multidisciplinary Journal is a peer-reviewed academic platform dedicated to fostering interdisciplinary research and dialogue.

The journal promotes original thinking, critical inquiry and innovative solutions to contemporary challenges across sciences, social sciences, humanities and professional studies. It is published by Shivaji College, University of Delhi.

“Academic work becomes more valuable when insight travels beyond the boundary of a single discipline.”

Double-blind peer review

Author and reviewer identities remain concealed. The editorial desk first checks scope, originality, research ethics and formatting before external review.

Publication ethics

Submissions must disclose conflicts of interest, funding and required approvals. Plagiarism, fabricated data and duplicate publication are not accepted.

Article format

Use a clear scholarly structure with consecutively numbered pages. Final articles must display journal title, volume, issue, month, year and page range on the first page.

Publication readiness

Designed around ISSN India requirements.

  • 01Consistent journal title across website, cover and articles
